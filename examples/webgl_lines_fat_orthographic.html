Orthographic world-unit lines: two separate errors

Each panel shows the same horizontal segment. Gold is rendered coverage; the white outline marks the expected width and endcaps. The camera looks along −Z. Moving the line sideways in camera space should not change its shape.

1. Dashed material — isolates the original extrusion error

One uninterrupted dash bypasses the solid-line distance test. The original shader makes the off-axis line too thin.

2. Solid material — exposes the vertex / fragment mismatch

The vertex-only fix expands the geometry correctly, but the fragment shader still measures distance to a perspective ray. Compare the body and both endcaps with the reference.

Segment: X = −2…2, Z = −2; width is adjustable above. Near = 0.1, far = 100. Expanded geometry stays at least 1.5 units in front of the camera: these errors are not near-plane clipping. The frustum follows Y to keep the line visible, without moving or rotating the camera.
