FAST spatiotemporal blue noise by Electronic Arts, https://github.com/electronicarts/fastnoise (see LICENSE.txt)

Source: noise.zip in that repository, 128x128x32 textures optimized for a sigma = 1 gaussian
spatially and an alpha = 0.1 exponential moving average temporally ("separate05" filter combine).
Separate (spatial weight 0.5) scores each filter on its own, so the noise is blue in space and also blue over time per pixel, converging much faster under temporal accumulation alone.

Packing: 256x4096 RGB PNG, 32 slices of 128 rows stacked vertically (slice t at y = t * 128).
Left 128 columns: R, G = noise/vector2/temporal/exp/vector2_uniform_gauss1_0_exp0101_separate05_t.png channels R, G
                  B = noise/real/temporal/exp/real_uniform_gauss1_0_exp0101_separate05_t.png channel R
Right 128 columns: R = G = B = the same real texture, circularly shifted 83 rows down and 49 columns right
Read with examples/jsm/tsl/utils/FASTNoise.js as fastNoise().xyzw.
